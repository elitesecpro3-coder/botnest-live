Frequently Asked Questions

Can BotNest answer questions about specific treatments like Botox or fillers?

Yes. During setup you provide details about your service menu — Botox, dermal fillers, laser treatments, chemical peels, body contouring, and more. BotNest is trained to describe your treatments, explain what clients can expect, and guide them toward booking a consultation.

Will BotNest book consultations directly or just collect inquiries?

BotNest collects the client’s name, phone number, and treatment interest, then can direct them to your booking link or submit a consultation request. Your team receives an instant email notification so you can follow up with every warm lead the next morning.

Can I customize BotNest for my med spa’s specific service menu?

Yes. BotNest is fully customized to your practice. During setup you describe your services, pricing approach, consultation process, and any pre-qualification questions you want asked. Every conversation reflects your brand’s voice and professionalism.

How does BotNest handle sensitive questions about medical aesthetic procedures?

BotNest handles treatment questions at the informational level appropriate for a website conversation. For detailed medical questions or contraindications, BotNest routes clients to your team, just as a professional receptionist would — while keeping them engaged rather than sending them to a competitor.

Will I receive instant notifications when a new client wants to book?

Yes. Every time a potential client engages with BotNest and provides their contact details, your designated email receives an instant notification with their name, phone number, and treatment interest. Your team starts every morning with a list of warm leads ready to convert into booked consultations.
