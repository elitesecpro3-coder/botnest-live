01

Recurring Revenue

Monthly subscriptions are the most durable revenue model in software. A white-label AI platform gives you a product to charge for every month — not one-time project fees that require constant new sales to maintain.

  • Predictable MRR over unpredictable project revenue
  • Higher business valuation on recurring revenue multiples

02

Client Retention

When a client runs their business on software you provide, they don't leave. A branded AI system creates platform dependency that increases lifetime value and makes churn expensive for the client — not just for you.

  • Clients on your platform stay longer than project clients

Platform Ownership

Reselling someone else's tool caps your margin and your control. Owning your infrastructure — even white-labeled — means you set pricing, own the client relationship, and keep the difference between cost and revenue.

  • No third-party pricing changes to absorb or pass on

  • Infrastructure asset that builds business equity

Common Questions About the Partnership

Is this fully white-label — no BotNest branding visible to clients?

Yes. The chat widget, branding layer, and all client-facing experiences carry your brand entirely. End clients see your agency or product name, your colors, and your logo. BotNest branding is not shown unless you choose to include it.

Can my agency resell this to clients under our own pricing?

Yes. The infrastructure is designed specifically for agency resale. You set your own pricing, contract terms, and service levels. Your clients pay you — not BotNest. The infrastructure arrangement is between BotNest and your agency.

How fully customizable is the branding on each deployment?

The widget name, logo, color scheme, and conversational messaging are customizable per deployment. Deeper customization including custom domain for the backend API and full white-label hosting arrangements are discussed during the strategy consultation.

Can the platform run multiple clients simultaneously?

Yes. The backend API is built for multi-tenant operation from the ground up. Each client deployment gets its own bot ID, isolated configuration, independent AI behavior, and separate notification routing. Adding new clients is a configuration step, not a rebuild.

Does the platform use OpenAI, and who manages API costs?

Yes. The AI engine uses OpenAI language models for all conversational responses. OpenAI API usage costs and infrastructure arrangements are discussed during the strategy consultation — including how costs are factored into your pricing structure.

Can the widget be installed on any type of website?

Yes. The widget is a JavaScript embed that installs with a single script tag. It works on WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Framer, and any custom HTML/CSS website. If a site can accept a script tag, the widget can be deployed on it.

Can features and integrations be added to the platform after launch?

Yes. The architecture is built with extension in mind. Additional AI behaviors, CRM integrations, booking system connections, and new features can be added as the platform grows. The expansion roadmap is part of the partnership discussion.

What does the strategy call cover, and what's the next step?

The 30-minute strategy call covers your agency or business model, how white-label AI SaaS would fit your service stack, the infrastructure arrangement terms, deployment timeline, and pricing structure.
